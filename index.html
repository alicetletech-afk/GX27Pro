PRELAUNCH QUALIFIERS→FINAL FOUR→W3 FINALS

Four finalist teams compete on GX27 Pro devices at the official launch, subject to game rights and device compatibility. A separate walk-in Solo Gaming Challenge supports additional trials.

RoV is proposed as a Thailand-first option for stronger local relevance, tournament culture and mass youth reach. Final selection depends on local rights, approval, cost and timeline. Fallback: creator-led community challenge focused on GX27 Pro performance without relying heavily on official game IP.
